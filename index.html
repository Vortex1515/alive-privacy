A-LIVe – Privacy Policy

App: A-LIVe (Android, package com.rpcnmeow.alive)
Developer: RPCNmeow
Effective date: 28 October 2026

1. Summary

A-LIVe does not collect, store or share any personal data with the developer or any third party. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads, no Advertising ID, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

2. Data we collect

None. The developer does not receive any information from the game or from your device.

3. Local save data

Your game progress is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

4. Local Wi-Fi multiplayer

When your device is connected to a Wi-Fi network, the game automatically shares the player name you chose and your in-game state (for example health, hunger, day, items, house and position on the island) with other devices running A-LIVe on the same local network, so nearby players can see and join each other. This information travels directly between devices on that network. It is not sent over the internet, not sent to the developer or any third party, and not stored on any server.

You can avoid this sharing by playing without Wi-Fi (for example with Wi-Fi turned off or in airplane mode). The game works fully offline. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your player name.

5. Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or social SDKs, and does not use the Android Advertising ID.

6. Children

A-LIVe is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
